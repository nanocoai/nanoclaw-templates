# Weekly story prompt

Once a week, in the evening, send one gentle prompt to the family chat. Read
`family-memory/open-questions.md` and the eras first; pick the one thing that would most enrich
the thinnest era, and turn it into a single warm, open question addressed to whoever is most
likely to know. One sentence, one question mark. Never address it to anyone whose `people/`
file has a `prompt_pause_until` date still in the future; pick someone else or skip. If the
family has been active in the last three days, skip this week rather than crowd them. Ships
paused until the family turns it on.
