# Monthly chapter draft

On the first of the month, draft one chapter from the era with the most confirmed and candidate
claims that has no draft yet, following the `story-keeper` skill's `drafting.md`. Write it to
`drafts/<era-slug>-<yyyy-mm-dd>.md`. Then send the family two or three sentences: which era, how
long the draft is, and the one open question that would most improve it. Do not paste the whole
draft into the chat unless asked. Ships paused until the family turns it on.
