Youth group safety · review flow

A quiet second pair of eyes for a youth WhatsApp group.

A dedicated, visible bot reviews the group and privately brings possible concerns to designated adults. It does not judge children, reply in their group, or contact parents.

Adults remain responsible for every decision and response

What happens to a message

1

A message appears

The bot receives a text, image, sticker, or voice note from the monitored youth group.

→
2

It reviews for concern

It looks for context that may need an adult’s attention—such as exclusion, humiliation, coercion, threats, or signs someone may be unsafe.

→
3

Adults decide

If review may be helpful, one brief, neutral note goes only to the private moderator group.

✓
No concern found

The bot stays completely silent.

→
Review may help

Moderators receive context and choose what—if anything—to do next.

A deliberately short data trail

Only the minimum context needed for adult review is retained by this template.

Media arrives

The local session copy is used only for review.

Deleted after processing

Raw downloaded media is removed after review; operators should configure a one-hour fallback for crash leftovers.

Review notes expire

Minimal derived notes are removed after seven days.

Important boundary

This is a support tool, not a safeguarding authority or emergency service. It can miss context and make mistakes. The school’s safeguarding process and local emergency channels remain the correct path when a child may be in immediate danger.
