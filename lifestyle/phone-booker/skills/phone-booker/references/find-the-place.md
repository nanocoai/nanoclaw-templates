# Find the Place

Turn the ask into a shortlist of up to three places, best first, each with a phone number, an
address, and opening hours.

## Memory first, always

Before you search anything: does `memory/places/` already have it? If they named a place you've
booked before, you already have its number, who to ask for, and what went wrong last time — use
it, and don't re-derive it from the web. If they said "the usual" or "same as last time", memory
is the only place that answer exists.

A place you know still gets its hours sanity-checked when the booking is unusual (a holiday, very
early, very late).

## Searching

When you do need to search, you're after four facts per candidate: **name, phone number, address,
hours**. A fifth matters: **do they take bookings by phone at all?** Some places are walk-in only,
some are online-only and will tell you to use the app. Note it when you see it — it saves the next
call.

Prefer the business's own site for the number over an aggregator; aggregators carry stale numbers
and forwarding services. When two sources disagree, take the one on the business's own page.

Push a broad sweep — "somewhere Italian in the area with outside seating" — to a subagent and have
it hand back just the shortlist. Keep this thread for judgment.

## Ordering the shortlist

Best first, and "best" is mostly about them, not about ratings:

1. **A place they've been to before** that fits the ask, always first. Memory beats a search
   result.
2. **A place that matches the standing asks** — if they always want an outside table, a place with
   no outside seating is a bad first call however good it is.
3. Then the ordinary signals: fits the window, right area, well reviewed.

Three is the cap. If you can't find three, that's fine — call the ones you have.

## When they named a specific place

Then there's no shortlist to build: find that place's number and go. Only build a shortlist if the
autonomy setting says handle it, so you have somewhere to fall back to when they're full — and say
you're doing that in the report, never silently.

## Record what you found

A place you rang goes into `memory/places/` whether or not the booking worked, with the number you
used and whether it was right. That's the whole point: the second time, this step is free.
