# Book It

Help the family make an appointment or reservation. You take it as far as the tools allow and hand off
the last step you can't do. Never claim a booking you can't point to a real reply for.

## Steps

1. **Nail down the ask**: what, when, for whom, and any constraints (time window, party size,
   budget, area). Fill gaps from the profile, or ask.
2. **Find the place and how to book**: web-search the spot and its booking path: an online link, an
   email, or a phone number, plus hours. If they named the place, just find its booking method.
3. **Take it as far as the tools allow.**
   - **Email-bookable** (many restaurants, dentists, contractors): draft the request or reservation
     email, show it, and send when action is approved.
   - **Online form** (live OpenTable/Resy flows): drive it with agent-browser: find the slot, fill
     the form, and stop before the final submit; show the family exactly what you're about to book
     and finish only on their go-ahead. If the flow demands a login, payment details, a CAPTCHA,
     or a verification code, tee everything up and hand them the link to finish.
   - **Phone only**: you can't call; hand them the number with the details to give.
4. **Hold the calendar**: when confirmed, create the event, confirm before you invite attendees.
5. **Track the confirmation**: you aren't woken when the reply lands, so schedule a one-time
   follow-up task to check the inbox (and check again on later runs if it hasn't arrived). When
   it lands, update the event to confirmed and tell the family. If it's declined or offers
   another time, bring it back to them.

## Output

```
For approval (before anything sends or submits)
> <the draft email, or what the form is about to book>

Once it's moving
📅 Held <event>: <date · time> (tentative until confirmed), <parent> invited
✅ Confirmed <event>: <date · time>   ← once the reply lands

Can't-finish handoff (login, payment, CAPTCHA, or phone)
Here's everything teed up; finish here: <link or number>, <details to give them>
```

Show the draft first and wait; only the "held / confirmed / handoff" lines follow, depending on how
the booking goes.
