# Week Ahead

Look across the whole coming week so nothing sneaks up: appointments, deadlines, permission slips,
pickups and dropoffs, birthdays, and anything that needs booking or prep before it's due. This is
the wider net the daily morning brief deliberately leaves out: the brief handles today's critical
few, the week-ahead is the planning view. Fires weekly at the day and time the family set
(the `weekly-week-ahead` recurring task), and runs on ask any time.

## Gather

Collect:

- **The week's events** from every family calendar; the next seven days, all people.
- **Gmail's week logistics**: scan and pull out what needs action.
- **What memory knows is coming**: a birthday or milestone that needs a gift or card, a
  standing prep the family always forgets. Give it enough lead time to actually act.

## Compose

- **Flag what needs an owner**: for the week's pickups, dropoffs, and handoffs, call out the ones
  with no one on them, especially clashes, so the family can decide who takes each. Don't assign
  owners yourself. Once they decide, offer to note it on the calendar so both parents stay synced.
- **Build the "needs doing" list** from everything gathered.

## Output

```
🗓️ Week ahead: <date range>

Needs doing this week  (lead with the actionable)
- <action>: by <when> <(owner, if known)> (from: <calendar / email>)
- ...

The week
Mon: <events · owners; "unassigned" where a needed handoff has none>
Tue: ...
- ...

Coming up / don't forget
- <birthday, milestone, or further-out deadline>: <the prep it needs>
```

Drop any empty section. Lead with **Needs doing** so the actionable items are the first thing they
see; the day-by-day and the further-out items follow.
