# Meals & Grocery

Plan the week's meals around the family's schedule, tastes, and allergies, build the grocery list,
and hunt the best prices for it. Fires the morning of the family's usual grocery-shopping day
(`tasks/weekly-meal-plan.md`), so they have the plan and list in hand before they shop — and runs on
ask any time.

**Dietary needs and allergies are hard rules.** Never plan a meal that breaks one; when unsure about
an ingredient, leave it out or ask.

**Never invent a deal.** Every price or sale you cite comes from a real web result with a source.
Nothing genuinely on sale? Say so — don't manufacture a deal to look useful.

## Steps

1. **Lean on the profile** — the food staples, dietary rules and allergies, likes and won't-eats,
   the regular stores, and the week's activities (busy or out-of-the-house nights).
2. **First grocery run: get the full list.** Onboarding only captured staples. The first time they
   ask for grocery help, build out their full shopping list with them and save it to your workspace;
   after that you maintain the running list instead of starting over.
3. **Plan the week's meals off real recipes.** Count the dinners actually needed — skip nights the
   calendar or activities say they're out or covered. For each, web-search an actual recipe that
   fits the dietary rules and the night's effort level, and **link it** so they can cook it. Lean on
   staples, vary it, and match effort to the night (quick meals on the busy ones). Offer it as a
   plan they can tweak, not a decree.
4. **Build the grocery list from the recipes.** Pull the ingredient quantities straight from the
   linked recipes (scaled to the family's size), plus staples running low — don't eyeball amounts.
   Group it so it's easy to shop — produce, dairy, pantry, and so on.
5. **Hunt the best prices** (the discount hunter). For the list, web-search the family's regular
   stores' current prices, sales, and weekly circulars; surface genuine savings — an item on sale
   this week, or cheaper at one store than another — with where to buy it. Only real, sourced deals;
   never manufacture a sale to look useful.
