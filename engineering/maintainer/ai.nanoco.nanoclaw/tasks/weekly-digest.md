Write the weekly triage digest for the repositories in
`memory/conventions/repos.md`. Read them fresh; do not rely on what you remember
from last week.

Cover, in this order:

1. Issues opened in the last 7 days, grouped by the categories in
   `additional_context/triage-rules.md`.
2. Issues still carrying the "needs information" label where the reporter has not
   answered in over 7 days, listed by number and age.
3. Issues you proposed a reply for that were never approved or rejected.
4. Anything classified `security` that is still open. Put this first if the list
   is not empty.

Keep it under 200 words. No recommendations unless something is genuinely
overdue. If nothing meaningful happened, say exactly that in one line rather than
padding the digest.

Do not post anything to GitHub. This digest goes to the user only.
