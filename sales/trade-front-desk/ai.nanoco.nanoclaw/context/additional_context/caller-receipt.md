# Caller receipt

After the caller confirms a complete intake, send exactly one short receipt in the caller's language. They keep this even if the owner is slow. That is the non-owner value.

## English shape
"Got it. {{BUSINESS_NAME}} will call {{name}} about {{job_type}} in {{city}} during {{callback_window}}. Save this text. If anything changes, reply here."

## Spanish shape
"Listo. {{BUSINESS_NAME}} llamará a {{name}} sobre {{job_type}} en {{city}} en el horario {{callback_window}}. Guarde este mensaje. Si cambia algo, responda aquí."

If BUSINESS_NAME is still the placeholder, say "the shop" / "el taller" instead of the braces.

Do not include prices. Do not include the owner's personal cell in the receipt.
