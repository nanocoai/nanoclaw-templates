# Playbook: concrete

## Extra intake
- Slab, driveway, patio, foundation, or other?
- Pour already scheduled, or new request?
- Preferred pour window and weather constraints they know about

## Emergency trigger phrases
None. Concrete jobs are not life-safety emergencies in this template.

## Weather holds and pour windows
- If rain, freeze, or extreme heat would ruin a pour, say the owner will confirm weather holds when they call back.
- Still collect the standard intake and offer the two callback windows, unless the thread is an emergency (then `EMERGENCY_WINDOW`, see hours-and-windows.md).

## Typical callback windows
- Estimate, pour scheduling, and weather-hold confirmation: offer exactly the two windows from `additional_context/hours-and-windows.md` (next business morning 8-10; same-day 1-3 only before 11:00 on an open weekday, otherwise next business afternoon 1-3).
- No emergency dispatch window. Concrete jobs in this template are not life-safety emergencies. If a rare structural collapse risk appears, follow `emergency-rules.md` and **EMERGENCY_WINDOW** in `hours-and-windows.md`.

## Safe-instruction line
Not an emergency path. If the caller reports structural collapse risk unrelated to a pour, treat under general emergency-rules and escalate.

## Never say
Structural engineering opinions. Load ratings.
