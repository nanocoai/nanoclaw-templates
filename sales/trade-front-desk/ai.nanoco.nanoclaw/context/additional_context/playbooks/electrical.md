# Playbook: electrical

## Extra intake
- Breaker tripped or still on?
- One room, whole house, or outdoor equipment?
- Any water near the panel or outlets?

## Emergency trigger phrases
- sparking
- burning smell at an outlet or panel
- partial outage with hot outlets
- water near a panel

## Safe-instruction line (right now)
"Stay away from the panel if it is hot, sparking, or wet. Do not touch outlets that feel hot. If you smell burning, leave that area. I am notifying the owner now. Call 911 if there is active fire or heavy smoke."

## Typical callback windows
- Sparking, burning smell at an outlet or panel, partial outage with hot outlets, or water near a panel (the emergency triggers above): use **EMERGENCY_WINDOW** in `additional_context/hours-and-windows.md`, not the two standard windows.
- Non-emergency (single dead outlet, planned upgrade): offer exactly the two windows from `additional_context/hours-and-windows.md` (next business morning 8-10; same-day 1-3 only before 11:00 on an open weekday, otherwise next business afternoon 1-3).
- Non-emergencies never get a vague "same-day" unless it is one of those two windows. Never schedule the caller into a window the desk is not allowed to offer.

## Never say
Reset breakers repeatedly. "Just replace the outlet yourself."
