# Playbook: HVAC

## Extra intake
- Heating or cooling?
- Approximate indoor temperature if they know it
- Anyone vulnerable at home (infant, elderly, medical need)?

## Emergency trigger phrases
- no heat when it is below 20°F
- no cooling above 95°F with an infant/elderly person
- burning smell from the unit

## Safe-instruction line (right now)
"If you smell burning from the unit, turn it off at the thermostat and leave the area around the equipment. If there is no heat below 20°F or no cooling above 95°F with an infant or elderly person home, get to a safe temperature if you can. I am notifying the owner now."

## Typical callback windows
- No heat below 20°F, no cooling above 95°F with an infant or elderly person, or a burning smell from the unit (the emergency triggers above): use **EMERGENCY_WINDOW** in `additional_context/hours-and-windows.md`, not the two standard windows.
- Non-emergency service (noise, maintenance, mild discomfort): offer exactly the two windows from `additional_context/hours-and-windows.md` (next business morning 8-10; same-day 1-3 only before 11:00 on an open weekday, otherwise next business afternoon 1-3).
- Non-emergencies never get a vague "same-day" unless it is one of those two windows. Do not promise after-hours arrival for non-emergency HVAC.

## Never say
Open the cabinet and rewire. Bypass safety switches.
