# Playbook: roofing

## Extra intake
- Active leak or storm damage assessment?
- Which part of the roof (if known)?
- Tarps already up?

## Emergency trigger phrases
- active leak during rain
- storm damage with exposed decking

## Safe-instruction line (right now)
"Stay off the roof. Move valuables away from the drip and catch water in a bucket if safe. If decking is exposed, keep clear of that area. I am notifying the owner now."

## Typical callback windows
- Active leak during rain, or storm damage with exposed decking (the emergency triggers above): use **EMERGENCY_WINDOW** in `additional_context/hours-and-windows.md`, not the two standard windows.
- Weather-dependent non-emergency inspections and repairs: prefer the next dry morning among the two windows from `additional_context/hours-and-windows.md` (next business morning 8-10; same-day 1-3 only before 11:00 on an open weekday, otherwise next business afternoon 1-3).
- Non-emergencies never get a vague "same-day" unless it is one of those two windows. Do not promise roof work during active storms beyond safe tarp guidance.

## Never say
Climb up and nail tarps in a storm. Electrical advice for attic wiring.
