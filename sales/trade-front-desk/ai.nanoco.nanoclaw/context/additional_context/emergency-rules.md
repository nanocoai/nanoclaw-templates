# Emergency rules (summary)

This file carries the cross-trade trigger list only. The playbooks own every safe-instruction sentence; quote them from `playbooks/<trade>.md`, never from here. Nothing in this file restates that wording.

## Always treat as emergency
- gas smell
- sewage backup
- water you cannot shut off
- carbon monoxide alarm
- no heat when it is below 20°F
- no cooling above 95°F with an infant/elderly person
- burning smell from the unit
- sparking
- burning smell at an outlet or panel
- partial outage with hot outlets
- water near a panel
- active leak during rain
- storm damage with exposed decking

## Safe-instruction line (right now)
Safe instruction: see `playbooks/<trade>.md` for the trade that owns the matched trigger, and quote its safe-instruction line verbatim. Do not paraphrase, reorder, or shorten it. Spanish twins of those same lines are in `spanish-intake.md`.

| Trigger group | Playbook that owns the line |
| --- | --- |
| gas smell, carbon monoxide alarm | `playbooks/plumbing.md` |
| sewage backup, water you cannot shut off | `playbooks/plumbing.md` |
| sparking, burning smell at an outlet or panel, partial outage with hot outlets, water near a panel | `playbooks/electrical.md` |
| no heat below 20°F, no cooling above 95°F with an infant/elderly person, burning smell from the unit | `playbooks/hvac.md` |
| active leak during rain, storm damage with exposed decking | `playbooks/roofing.md` |

## Never
- DIY repair steps for gas, electrical, or CO
- "It can wait until morning" when an emergency phrase matched
- Dialing anyone except the owner placeholders

Escalate with the emergency-escalate skill.
