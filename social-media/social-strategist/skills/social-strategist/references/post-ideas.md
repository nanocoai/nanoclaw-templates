# Post Ideas

Ranked post ideas for the platforms they ask about, based on what has worked for this creator,
their niche and their goal. Each idea is a hook, a format and a topic, with the evidence behind
it.

## Steps

1. **Ground.** Read the `brand-profile`: niche, audience, goal, `topics`, off-limits topics,
   voice, posting languages. For each platform asked, read `platform-<x>` (what works, what
   doesn't, n). Read the `post-log` topic scores and the `content-log` so you don't repeat an
   idea already offered, scripted or posted, and don't bring back one they rejected unless the
   reason no longer applies. No platform named? Use every platform in the profile, since the
   profile answers that question.

2. **Generate candidates.** Aim for about twice as many as you will show. Favor the topics and
   formats that are winning on the goal's headline metric (router → Definitions). **About 1 in 5
   is a deliberate test** of an angle, format or topic they haven't tried yet. Label it `test` so
   a flop doesn't count against their usual formats.

3. **Timely angle (optional, built-in web search).** When the niche has news or a trend worth
   using, run one or two searches for something from the last 30 days. An idea built on that gets
   the `timely` label and its source link. If nothing recent fits, leave it out rather than
   stretch an old story.

4. **Rank** by expected fit with the goal: proven topic + proven format first, then proven topic
   in a new format, then tests. Push back in the "why" line when an idea they asked for has weak
   evidence behind it.

5. **Save.** Add every idea you offered to the `content-log`, one line each:
   `<yyyy-mm-dd> · idea · <platform> · <format> · <topic> · "<hook>" · <test|timely|-> · offered`.
   When they react, change `offered` to `accepted` or `rejected: <their reason>`. A reason that
   says something lasting ("never do dance trends", "I don't show my face") goes into the
   `brand-profile` as well.

## Output

One block per platform, 3–5 ideas each.

```
💡 LinkedIn ideas · goal: leads

1. "You're undercharging, here's proof."
   text post · pricing
   why: pricing posts: 3.1% median vs your 2.4% overall (n=6, directional)
2. "I lost a client by saying yes too fast."
   document carousel · client work · test
   why: you haven't tried carousels yet; your client-story posts lead on comments (n=4, directional)
3. "The new freelancer tax rule, in 60 seconds."
   text post · taxes · timely
   why: your explainer posts get 1.8x comments (n=5, directional) · source: <link>

Want a script for one? Reply with its number.
```

Before sending:

- Hook: a real first line or opening sentence, not a topic label.
- Format is one they can make with their filming setup. If the setup is unknown, prefer formats
  that need none (text post, carousel).
- Why cites evidence from `platform-<x>` or `post-log`.
- At least one idea per block is labeled `test`. Every `timely` idea has its source link.
- Nothing already in the `content-log` as posted or rejected.
- Last line is the script offer.
