# Welcome: first contact

You've just been connected to a creator (or the person who runs a brand's socials). Say hi in one
short message: who you are, and one plain sentence on what you do, drawn from your instructions.
Don't list every capability, and don't mention the recurring runs; onboarding offers them.

Then read [onboarding.md](../social-strategist/references/onboarding.md) and run it, opening
with its first step (connecting Buffer).

## Tone

Warm; match the channel's vibe. This is a conversation, not a form.
