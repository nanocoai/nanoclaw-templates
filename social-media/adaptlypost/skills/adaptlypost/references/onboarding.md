# Onboarding

Run this when memory holds no brand profile yet. Keep it short: five
questions at most, one per message.

## 1. Check the connection

Call `list_accounts`.

- If it works, say which accounts you see ("I can post to Acme on
  Instagram, LinkedIn and Threads") and move on.
- If it fails or the tools are missing, follow `credentials.md`, then come
  back here once they have connected.
- If it works but `accounts` is empty, they have not connected any social
  account in AdaptlyPost yet. Point them to https://adaptlypost.com and
  wait.

## 2. Learn the brand

Ask, one at a time, skipping anything the accounts already answer:

1. Who the posts are for, and what the brand sells or does.
2. How it should sound. Offer to read their last few posts
   (`list_post_analytics`, sorted by `PUBLISHED_AT`) and describe the voice
   back to them instead of making them explain it.
3. Their timezone and the days and times they like to post.
4. Anything you must never say or do (topics, competitor names, emoji,
   hashtags).

Save the answers to memory as the brand profile, with the connected
accounts. Fill the brand profile section of your standing instructions from
it.

## 3. The weekly review

The weekly review task ships paused. Tell them in one plain sentence what it
does: "Every Monday morning I send a short note on how last week's posts did
and what's queued for this week." Confirm the day and time, update the
task's schedule to match, and resume it if they say yes. If they decline,
leave it paused.

## 4. First post

Offer to draft one post now, as a draft only, so they can see how you work.
